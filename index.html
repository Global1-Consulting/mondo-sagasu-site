About Mondo

More than a model. A creative vision.

Mondo Sagasu is a Tanzanian creative entrepreneur with a passion for fashion, dance and self-expression. His work combines the visual storytelling of fashion modeling, the energy of dance and the drive behind his own clothing business.

Through social media and digital platforms, Mondo shares his creative journey and builds connections with audiences, fellow creatives and brands worldwide. His vision is to grow his international presence, collaborate with innovative brands and create opportunities through fashion, entertainment and entrepreneurship.
